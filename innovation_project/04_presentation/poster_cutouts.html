Meet the salamander
The Jefferson Salamander is endangered in Ontario.
Where it lives
It needs both forests and ponds to live.
When they cross
On cool, rainy spring nights, they walk from the forest to the pond.
The danger
Stouffville Road is in the way, and cars are a big danger.
The tunnels
Tunnels were put under the road in 2025 so salamanders can cross safely.
Fences and cameras
Fences guide salamanders to the tunnels, and cameras watch for animals.
Studied for years
TRCA has studied salamanders at Stouffville Road since 2002.
Our problem
Are salamanders using the tunnels, or turning back?
