# Jayden · Identify

**Hook**

Did you know the Jefferson Salamander is endangered in Ontario?

**Body**

On cool, rainy spring nights, they walk from the forest to the pond, but Stouffville Road is in the way, and cars are a big danger to them.

We visited Stouffville Road, where TRCA has studied salamanders since 2002.

Tunnels were put under the road so salamanders can cross safely.

Fences guide them to the tunnels, and cameras watch for animals going in.

But something the experts still need to know is if salamanders are actually using the tunnels, because they could be turning back.
