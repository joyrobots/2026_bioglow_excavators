## My script

**Hook**

Did you know the Jefferson Salamander is endangered in Ontario?

**Say, then show**

On cool, rainy spring nights, they walk from the forest to the pond, but Stouffville Road is in the way, and cars are a big danger to them.

We did research at Stouffville Road and saw the setup that is already there.

Tunnels were put under the road in 2025, so salamanders can cross safely.

Fences guide them to the tunnels.

Cameras and pressure plates watch for animals going in.

But something we still need to know is if salamanders are actually using the tunnels, because they could be turning back.

**Hand-off**

Next, Lucas will explain our design.
