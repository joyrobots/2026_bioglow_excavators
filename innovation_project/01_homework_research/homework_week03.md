### **📚 What We Learned in Class**

This week we went over the basics of **GitHub**, the online "vault" where our team saves code, notes and ideas so everyone can see them and nothing gets lost. A **repository (repo)** is our team's project folder, and a **commit** is saving your work with a short note about what you changed.

### **🚨 Part 1 Mission: Put Your First File in the Team Vault\!**

With a parent, upload **one file (anything you like\!)** into our team folder `week03_gitupload/`.
*Ideas: a photo of your Week 1 animal sketch, your robot or LEGO build, or a text file saying hi to the team.*

**Rule:** Start your file name with your first name so nobody overwrites anyone else *(e.g., `Hugh's_robot_sketch.jpg`)*.

### **🔍 Steps**

1. **Sign in** to GitHub and go to **github.com/joyrobots/2026_bioglow_excavators**
   Then click the folders: **week03_gitupload**
2. Click **"Add file"** → **"Upload files"** (top right, next to the green **Code** button).
3. **Drag your file** into the box, or click **"choose your files"**.
4. In the **"Commit changes"** box, write a short note *(e.g., `Add Hugh's robot sketch`)*.
5. Click the green **"Commit changes"** button. 
6. Go back to the **week03_gitupload** folder. Can you see your file? ✅
